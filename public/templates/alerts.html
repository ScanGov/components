Confirmation alerts

The standard way to tell a user an action succeeded, failed, or needs their attention — after a form submits, an email sends, or a flow completes. Always a Font Awesome icon, never an emoji, so the message stays legible to screen readers and consistent across platforms/fonts.

Success

.alert-success with a leading fa-solid icon (a single space, no margin utility, between the icon and the message — the alert box provides its own padding). Put a follow-up action as an inline link inside the message when it reads naturally as a sentence, or as a .btn below the alert when the action deserves its own emphasis.

Help is on the way! We'll follow up shortly.
Welcome to ScanGov. Let's get your first site scanned.
Add a site 
<!-- Action inline in the message -->
<div class="alert alert-success" role="alert">
  <i class="fa-solid fa-life-ring" aria-hidden="true"></i> Help is on the way! We'll follow up shortly.
</div>

<!-- Action as a button below -->
<div class="alert alert-success" role="alert">
  <i class="fa-solid fa-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i> Welcome to ScanGov. Let's get your first site scanned.
</div>
<a href="/scan" class="btn btn-primary">Add a site</a>

Info

.alert-info for a neutral heads-up that isn't the result of an action — a hint, a pointer to docs, a note about what's coming next.

Your answer may be in the docs. 
<div class="alert alert-info" role="alert">
  <i class="fa-solid fa-file-lines" aria-hidden="true"></i> Your answer may be in the docs.
</div>

Danger

.alert-danger for a failed action. Pair with a way to retry — either a link back to the form or a "Try again" button.

Something went wrong sending your request. Please try again. 
<div class="alert alert-danger" role="alert">
  <i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation" aria-hidden="true"></i> Something went wrong sending your request. Please try again.
</div>
